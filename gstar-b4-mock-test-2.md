## Summary

- **Five items cannot be answered as printed, and one has two answers.**
  - Part 1 Q2–3 have no answer in the audio (K01).
  - Part 14 Q5–6 do not match their recording: Q6 asks about a man and a project that the dialog never mentions (K04, K05).
  - Part 7 Q39/Q40 accept either order (K06).
- **Two names contradict the audio:** the sender is `Avila` in print and `Rogers` in the audio (K02); `Ferb` in Q24–25 is `Finn` in the audio (K03).
- **Eleven test-book faults.** The most serious is the Part 14 score box: it uses a five-criteria rubric for a part scored one point per answer (01). The answer sheet also asks for one-word answers where Part 1 needs two words (03–04).
- **Six audioscript faults**, including `Lui` against the printed `Louie` (A01).
- **Conformance:** all 40 SB4 L5–8 words appear, and all eight grammar points are used. Forward references are all editorial: *could have* (SB6) heard once; the modal passive and *which* clauses (SB5) read several times, once in a key.
- **25 editorial candidates**, including Ménière's disease for a schoolchild (E12) and the Part 12 opening sentence printed away from the answer lines (E14).
