# Control: G-Star Student Book 5 answer key, final (`G star SB B5-key.pdf`)

Hand proofread on 1 October 2026 of the Student Book 5 answer key, the last check before it goes
to the printer. The model step was done by hand. I read every page of the key at 110 dpi and
zoomed to 200–300 dpi for every footnote marker, picture and overlap listed below. Nothing paid
ran.

The key is the Student Book with the answers overlaid: red for keyed answers, blue for sample
answers (`Answers will vary.`). So it was checked two ways: every key against its item, and the
pages themselves as a proof of the book. The listening pages were checked against the audioscript
`5 G-Star SB5-audio.docx`, which arrived the same day. Every printed listening item was solved
from it (see "Listening").
