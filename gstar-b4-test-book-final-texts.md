**How it was checked.** I read every passage, dialogue, script turn and model answer myself, as a
piece of writing a student will read or copy. Nothing paid ran. Page numbers are printed pages.

**Overall.** The texts are much tighter than in October:
- The L1 cookie story no longer has Andrew taste food he thinks is bad.
- The L4 survey quotes now match its six-hour question.
- The L5 review's numbers add up.
- The L6 food-poisoning passage separates causes from signs.
- The L7 blog was replaced by a clear, well-paced story.

What is left is mostly one weak argument (L4), a few tense slips, and **three key model answers
that do not do what their own brief asks** (in the overall report: TB4F-23 to -26).
