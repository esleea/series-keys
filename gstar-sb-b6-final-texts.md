**Method.** Every page was read as an image. Four readers each took a quarter of the book (cover–L2,
L3–Review 1, L5–L6, L7–back matter), and four independent reviewers re-checked every must-fix
against the page images and the manuscript. Findings the reviewers rejected are dropped; findings
they judged real but minor are listed under "Should fix". Nothing paid ran.

**What was judged.** Every Opening, Usage, Reading and audio text in L1–L8, and the texts in
Reviews 1 and 2:
- does it read like a real text of its kind (genre, register);
- do the paragraphs flow, and does the story or argument hold together;
- repetition;
- level against the series target, **B1**, and fit for readers aged 11–12.

Every repair keeps the lesson's target grammar and vocabulary. Where a text fault is also a sentence
error, the full row is in `gstar-sb-b6-final.md`; here it is listed once with "see report 1".

Ids are `SB6F-Tnn`. **Must fix** means wrong as printed (a contradiction, an unsolvable puzzle, a
wrong fact, or the lesson's own grammar misused in its model text). **Should fix** means a
recommendation.
