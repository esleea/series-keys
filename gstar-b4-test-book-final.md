before print. This is the overall report, one of three: it covers keys, sentences, audio and the
October findings. The other two are `gstar-b4-test-book-final-texts.md` and
`gstar-b4-test-book-final-grammar.md`. The last checks, on 21 and 22 October

had no key; this round has one.

**Verdict: not ready to print, because of the key.** The book itself is much better than in
October: nearly every item that had two answers or none is fixed. But **the answer key has 15
wrong entries**. Most sit where the book changed after October and the key did not follow: L3,
L4, L7 Reading Part 2 and L8. Two listening gaps have no answer in the new audio. In all, 28 things
must change before print.
