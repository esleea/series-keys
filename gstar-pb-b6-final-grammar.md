# Control: G-Star Power Book 6, final print — grammar

Hand proofread on 5 November 2026 of G-Star Power Book 6. A Power Book has no grammar boxes of its own, so this report asks whether each lesson's work on the two points taught in SB6 is right: the lesson's own grammar used correctly, exercises that test one thing, and texts that show each point in a form the student can see. Sentence errors and keys are in `gstar-pb-b6-final.md`. Texts as writing are in `gstar-pb-b6-final-texts.md`.
