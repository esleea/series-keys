**Method.**
- Four readers each took a quarter of the book and did all three passes on it: sentences, texts, and grammar explanations.
- Every item was solved. The listening items were solved from the manuscript audioscripts.
- Four independent reviewers then checked each must-fix against the page image and the manuscript. They re-solved every key and re-read every page for misses.
- **The reviewers rejected no must-fix as wrong.**
- **They downgraded 8 must-fix items in this report** to recommendations or open questions. Examples are style points such as `works a demanding job` and `on sale` for a house, and the missing full stops after blanks.
- **They changed 4 fixes** (FC-02, FC-05, FD-K02, FC-21 in the working files).
- **They dropped 6 recommendations** because the change would have made the text worse.
- **They added 23 missed items.** Two are must-fix: SB6F-K01 and SB6F-13.

**The print is newer than the manuscript.** Most of the October fixes are in the print but not in
the manuscript. "In manuscript: yes" means the fault is in both files. "Print only" means only the
print is wrong. "Differs" means the two files have different wording. The audioscripts are not printed.
An audio fault therefore means a re-recording, if the recording follows the manuscript.

**Companion files.**
- `gstar-sb-b6-final-texts.md`: the Openings, Usages, Readings and audio texts judged as writing.
- `gstar-sb-b6-final-grammar-explanations.md`: the grammar bands, Grammar Notes, the pp71–72 summary wording, and whether each text models its lesson's grammar.

Ids are `SB6F-nn`. **Must fix** means wrong as printed. **Should fix** means a recommendation.
★ means the lesson's own target structure is used wrongly.
