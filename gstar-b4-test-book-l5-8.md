## Summary

- **6 items without exactly one right answer** (K08–K13). The worst: **L5 Listening Q9 has no correct option**, because the key says *younger sister* where the audio says *little brother* (K08). **Two L7 T/F items hinge on untaught *might* vs *would*** (K09). Three L6/L7 picture sets each have two pictures that fit (K10–K12).
- **9 audioscript faults.** The **L5 recording names a different book club** from the page (A13). **Names on three pages are never said in the audio** (A14). **L8's own word *keep up with* is misused, and the misuse is the answer to gap 2** (A15).
- **14 test-book faults.** These include **the wrong person, "Mia", in two L7 T/F statements** (16), a 5-in-1 set that holds six items (17), an L8 *why* question answered with pictures of *what* (18), and **an L5 Writing brief that never asks for the lesson's passive continuous** (19).
- **Conformance:** every lesson tests its own grammar, and all 40 words are used. However, the L5 passage never shows the passive continuous (C07), **L7 Writing has no grammar requirement** (C09), and L8 never scores indirect objects (C10). One forward reference is in a key option: the present perfect continuous in L6 (C08).
