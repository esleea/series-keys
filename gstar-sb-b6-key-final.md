| SB6K-01 | 15 | L2 Reading CQ1 `What is a smoke detector used for?` | `It is used to detect smokes in case of fire.` | *Smoke* is uncountable | `It is used to detect smoke and warn people about a possible fire.` |
| SB6K-02 | 15 | L2 Reading CQ2 `Explain why it's reasonable and important for humans to overreact…` | `Because in most cases, we might not be aware of how much danger we are in, so overreacting and getting ourselves to safety is less risky than not reacting at all.` | *Because … so* is a double conjunction (the L1 因為…所以 pattern). | SB6K-03 | 16 | L2 Speaking Ex2, C `mean` (Nathan) | `have his books be hidden away` | Not an action of Nathan's, and ungrammatical in the frame: `*It is mean of Nathan to have his books be hidden away.` | `hide his classmate's books` |

| SB6K-07 | 33 | Review 1 Ex2 Q4 `"I'll eat all the cake if you don't hurry," my sister said.` | `My sister threatened to eat all the cake if I don't hurry.` | No backshift after a past reporting verb | `… if I didn't hurry.` |
