## Method and scope

What was done, by hand, in this session:

- **Every page image was read** (rendered at 130 dpi). All 29 images came back. The copyright
  line was also read from a 400 dpi render. The text layer holds only the red and blue answers;
  every quoted answer was checked against it.
- **Every Practice item (E-A, E-B) was solved** from its options or word bank and compared with
  the key.
- **Every C answer was checked against its B text**, and every C callout against what B does.
- **Every blue sample (F plan and G text) was checked** against the lesson's own callouts, goals and
  H rubric, and F against G.
- **Every model and sample was read as writing**: sentence errors, paragraph links, story logic,
  repetition.
- **Every H rubric was added up.**
- **Regression:** every must-fix from the first-draft report
  ([`gstar-b4-writer-pre-mock-1.md`](gstar-b4-writer-pre-mock-1.md),
  W4D-01 to W4D-58) was checked against this version.
- **Grammar order:** the grammar in each new model was compared with the SB4 transcription
  files (`data/transcriptions/gstar-sb-4`; files 5 and 10 are reviews, so printed L5–L8 = files 6–9).

What was **not** done:

- Words were **not** checked against the core. I looked up about 50 words from the new models in
  `data/series/g-star-lexical-resolution.json`. Most are not listed there, and a missing word is
  no evidence of a fault, so no word-level findings are counted.
- **No review agents ran** on this report, and nothing paid ran.
- The live `/rd/series/g-star` core page was not opened.

**Ids:** `W4K-nn`. **Must fix** means a wrong key, an error in the English, a contradiction, a
sample that breaks the lesson's own rule, or a fault already reported and still open. **Should
fix** means a recommendation.
