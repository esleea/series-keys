## 1. What was checked

1. **Every fixed-answer Practice item:** All three questions in Exercise A and all three in Exercise B in each of eight lessons were solved independently (48 answer entries), and the printed red answers were checked against the wording and available options/word banks.
2. **Every Analyze organizer:** The red entries on printed pages 2, 6, 10, 14, 18, 22, 26, and 30 were compared with their corresponding Observe texts, not assumed correct because they were already in red.
3. **Every writing sequence:** The blue Brainstorm plans and blue Write model compositions were compared for content consistency, language, and whether the model meets the lesson's stated objectives and H/Check rubric.
4. **All surrounding page content:** The B/Observe examples, D/Word Corner examples, instructions, section labels, Overview, table of contents, and back-page copyright text were reviewed. Image-to-text consistency and obvious layout/readability concerns were also considered.
5. **Curriculum:** The G-Star repository's level-3 page/core records, authored grammar decisions, lexical-resolution data, and earlier Writer 3 mock-up QC report were consulted. The known 30 September vocabulary/grammar policy decisions were used only where explicit.

**Important qualification:** The live `/rd/series/g-star?tab=core` application was *not* independently opened; the available repository snapshots and previous editorial decisions were used. A word's absence from a vocabulary-headword list is not, by itself, proof that its use is prohibited. This report does not certify every incidental word against a complete, live, cumulative series curriculum. Nor does a content proofread substitute for a printer's technical preflight.

### Final disposition

**The supplied `GSTAR-WB3 ANS KEY.pdf` is *not print-ready*.** The result is stronger than the first pass, but it is not logically possible to grant a clean, final **print clearance** to a file that still contains known wrong answer keys and other correctable defects.
